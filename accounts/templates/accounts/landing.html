How it works

From a storeroom shelf to a new lab in four steps.

  1. ListA custodian registers a surplus item and publishes it to the catalogue.
  2. RequestAnother department finds it, with AI suggesting the best matches.
  3. ApproveAn approver who did not ask for it decides, so no one approves their own request.
  4. ReceiveThe item is handed over and custody changes only when the receiver confirms.
AI matching

The right item, found before you finish typing.

Describe what you need and the system ranks catalogue items by how well they fit, explaining each match. You choose; the AI never decides for you.

Assistant

Ask in plain words, from any page.

"Are there any laptops I can request?" The assistant answers from the live catalogue and your own requests, links straight to the items, and remembers your earlier conversations.

Sustainability

Every reuse counted, with the method shown.

The dashboard turns completed transfers into kilograms of CO2e avoided using EPA WARM factors, so the numbers can be checked, not just admired.
