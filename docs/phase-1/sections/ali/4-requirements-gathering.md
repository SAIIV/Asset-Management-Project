# 4 Requirements Gathering

### Evidence collected

The current evidence consists of the full project description and the Phase 1 guidance. Document analysis establishes the required system capabilities and report structure. It does not establish campus approval authority, current staff practices or the availability of institutional data. [R1, pp. 7–9; R2, pp. 9–10]

| Finding from document analysis | Requirement implication | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The system must support resource discovery and exchange | Register available assets, capture needs, prevent unavailable reservations and confirm transfers | R1, pp. 3–4; FR-02 to FR-04 |
| Records must cover the asset life cycle | Preserve inspections, repairs, custody changes and final actions | R1, pp. 3–4; FR-05 |

### Planned validation activities

The proposed gathering sequence is instructor consultation, an interview with an asset custodian or approved proxy, and a scenario walkthrough. Instructor consultation will establish the required prototype depth and identify an appropriate operational contact. The interview will document how assets are published, requested, approved, released, received and removed from service. The walkthrough will test that description against the monitor example and the exception cases in Section 2.

| Question to investigate | Intended respondent | Required output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who can approve release, transfer, donation, recycling and disposal? | Custodian and authorized approval representative, or approved proxy | Action-by-role permission matrix |
| Which asset fields are mandatory before publication? | Custodian and maintenance representative | Field definitions, mandatory values and condition rules |
| When does a reservation expire, and how are competing requests resolved? | Custodian and department representative | Reservation and cancellation rules |
| What confirms physical release and receipt? | Releasing and receiving representatives | Required confirmation fields and exception handling |
| Which inputs can support cost and environmental estimates? | Finance and sustainability representatives, or approved proxy | Input definitions, access restrictions and calculation sources |

Each recorded finding will identify its source, date, related requirement ID and decision. Conflicting answers will remain unresolved until the responsible stakeholder clarifies them. Proxy responses will be distinguished from direct staff evidence. No interview findings are reported at this stage.

### Requirement priorities

High-priority requirements establish a complete controlled exchange: authentication, accurate listings, search and reservation, approval, receipt and history. Classification and resource matching are also high priority because they support the comparison between conventional and AI-assisted discovery. Medium-priority functions are scheduled after this workflow but remain in scope. These include notifications, operational reporting, sustainable-action advice, conversational assistance and generated summaries.
